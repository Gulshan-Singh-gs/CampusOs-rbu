5. Critical Warnings
⚠️ NEVER bypass RLS in the Worker. Even though you have service role key, ALWAYS validate permissions at application layer FIRST. Service role is for system operations only, not user requests.
️ DO NOT enable Realtime on conversations or users tables. Only messages needs realtime. Broadcasting conversation updates creates unnecessary load and privacy risks.
️ Retention policies MUST be configurable. Hardcoding DELETE WHERE created_at < NOW() - INTERVAL '30 days' will cause compliance issues when university policy changes. Store retention days in conversations.metadata or admin settings.
⚠️ Test with REAL authenticated users. Fake messages hide RLS bugs, spam vulnerabilities, and realtime delivery failures. Seed data is acceptable ONLY if clearly marked as dev-only and isolated from production.
