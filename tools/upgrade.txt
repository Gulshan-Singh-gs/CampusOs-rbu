Comprehensive UX Audit & Operational Workflow ReportProduct: Campus Passport & Digital ResumeTarget URL: [https://campusos-rbu-owv.pages.dev/passport](https://campusos-rbu-owv.pages.dev/passport)Evaluation Role: Lead UX Engineer / User Experience AuditDate: October 07, 20261. Executive SummaryCore Question AssessmentCan a real student, recruiter, faculty member, or university administrator understand, trust, use, and complete their goal on this page without confusion or risk?Verdict: No. Across all four user groups, the product in its current state fails to provide an understandable, trustworthy, or functional experience.Key FindingsSevere Mental Model Mismatch: The interface conflates a public resume, a secure institutional ID card, an academic transcript, and a cryptographic credential wallet into a single unstructured view without establishing context, permission boundaries, or viewer identity.Unauthenticated Exposure of High-Stakes PII: Academic standing (GPA 8.8 / 10), attendance metrics (92%), full institutional UID (RBU21CSE045), and student identity are rendered unconditionally to any anonymous visitor without consent toggles, authentication gates, or role-based views.Dead Ends & Broken Interactive Affordances: Primary interactive triggers (Add Skill, Add First Skill) fail silently upon click, providing zero feedback, forms, or status transitions.Fabricated Trust Signals ("Security Theater"): Phrases such as "Authenticated via Rayat Bahra University CampusOS Zero-Trust Identity" and "Document Hash: SHA256-RBU-PASSPORT-2026" represent static, non-functional text strings. To technical evaluators and recruiters, these undermine credibility rather than establish it; to non-technical users, they introduce cognitive friction.Immediate Priority: Transition the product from an ambiguous, public static mockup to an authenticated, role-aware system with explicit privacy controls, working interaction loops, and verifiable institutional trust proofs.2. Research and Evaluation MethodologyThis evaluation was conducted using an expert cognitive walkthrough and rigorous heuristic inspection framework tailored to academic credentialing systems:First-Impression & Cognitive Walkthrough (0–10s): Evaluated immediate comprehension, visual hierarchy, trust markers, and initial emotional resonance across new sessions.Mental Model & Conceptual Mapping: Audited alignment between system claims (cryptographic zero-trust verification) and observable user actions.Task-Based Scenario Walkthroughs: Ran 10 specific user tasks representing core user journeys for students, recruiters, faculty, and anonymous visitors.Nielsen Norman 10 Usability Heuristics: Scored interface states against established human-computer interaction standards.Content & Language Usability Audit: Analyzed the cognitive load, jargon, and readability of all user-facing copy and empty states.Accessibility (a11y) & Cognitive Load Audit: Assessed semantic markup, color contrast, keyboard focus, and screen-reader operability.3. Product Understanding & Mental Model AnalysisWhat the Product Appears to BeThe application attempts to serve four conflicting functions simultaneously:A Digital Student ID: Displaying a badge, student name, institutional affiliation, and roll number/UID.An Official Academic Transcript: Exposing cumulative GPA and attendance percentages.An Interactive Career Portfolio / Resume: Hosting skills, endorsements, and an "About" statement.A Web3 / Cryptographic Verification Certificate: Displaying zero-trust identity claims and cryptographic hashes. Authentication Gap: The interface does not distinguish between a student managing their profile, a recruiter evaluating an applicant, and an unauthenticated public crawler. No session indicators, sign-in buttons, or profile switches exist.Privacy & Data Governance Gap: Academic metrics protected under student privacy standards (such as FERPA or equivalent regional data protection norms) are publicly accessible. The student cannot selectively redact their GPA, hide their attendance, or restrict access via password/token.Action & Transaction Gap: Every call-to-action is a dead end. There is no client-side state change, no validation dialog, and no pathway to submit data to an administrative review queue.Feedback & Status Gap: When actions fail, the interface fails silently. The user is given no indication whether a service is down, permissions are missing, or the product is under development.Verification & Provenance Gap: The platform claims zero-trust verification but provides no audit trail, issuing authority signature, or cryptographic verification interface.9. Heuristic Evaluation (Nielsen Norman Framework)H1: Visibility of System StatusObservation: The page gives no indication of user role, data freshness, synchronization status with university records, or system connectivity.User Impact: High confusion; users cannot tell if they are editing, viewing, or encountering network issues.Severity: Sev 1Recommendation: Add a contextual header bar: "Viewing as Guest • Last synced with RBU Registrar: October 05, 2026 • Read-Only".H2: Match Between System and the Real WorldObservation: High-context technical jargon ("Zero-Trust Identity", "Document Hash") is presented alongside casual resume conventions ("Add First Skill").User Impact: Non-technical recruiters and students find the terms confusing and alienating, while technical reviewers recognize them as misleading.Severity: Sev 1Recommendation: Use standard academic terminology: "Verified by Registrar", "Institutional Record", and replace the raw hash with "Digital Signature & Audit Trail".H3: User Control and FreedomObservation: The student owner has zero control over profile visibility, section toggling, or data privacy.User Impact: Severe loss of agency; users feel exposed and vulnerable.Severity: Sev 0Recommendation: Provide profile editing controls with visibility toggles for GPA, attendance, and contact information.H4: Consistency and StandardsObservation: The primary call-to-action (Add Skill) looks like an active button but behaves like plain decorative text.User Impact: Violates fundamental web UI conventions; breeds immediate distrust.Severity: Sev 0Recommendation: Ensure all clickable elements adhere to strict affordance standards: functional click handlers, loading states, and disabled styling when inactive.H5: Error PreventionObservation: Unauthenticated visitors are allowed direct access to sensitive student profiles with no confirmation prompts or warnings about public visibility.User Impact: High data leak risk for students sharing links casually.Severity: Sev 0Recommendation: Warn students prior to generating public links and require explicit confirmation to expose academic metrics.H6: Recognition Rather Than RecallObservation: The student's UID (RBU21CSE045) is displayed without explaining what the alphanumeric code signifies (e.g., Rayat Bahra University, 2021 Batch, Computer Science Engineering).User Impact: External recruiters cannot decipher institutional codes without external research.Severity: Sev 2Recommendation: Expand internal university codes: "Batch of 2021 • B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering • Roll No: 045".H7: Flexibility and Efficiency of UseObservation: A single static view is forced upon all user segments. There is no fast-track verification for recruiters and no management dashboard for students.User Impact: Low efficiency across all workflows.Severity: Sev 2Recommendation: Introduce segmented view modes: a one-click "Recruiter Summary View" and a comprehensive "Full Transcript & Credential View".H8: Aesthetic and Minimalist DesignObservation: The footer is cluttered with pseudo-cryptographic assertions that serve no user utility.User Impact: Clutters visual hierarchy with unhelpful technical noise.Severity: Sev 2Recommendation: Consolidate cryptographic verification into an accessible verification badge with an on-demand details drawer.H9: Help Users Recognize, Diagnose, and Recover from ErrorsObservation: Dead buttons produce zero error messages, warnings, or explanatory tooltips.User Impact: Users assume their device, browser, or mouse is malfunctioning.Severity: Sev 1Recommendation: If a feature is in development or requires authentication, display an explanatory modal or disabled tooltip explaining prerequisite steps.H10: Help and DocumentationObservation: The page provides no FAQ, support links, registrar contact info, or onboarding guidance.User Impact: Users with questions or data discrepancies have no recourse.Severity: Sev 2Recommendation: Include an accessible footer support link: "Discrepancy in records? Accessibility (a11y) & Responsiveness AuditAccessibility as a User Experience DriverSemantic Hierarchy: The page uses non-semantic div tags for headings and metric displays. Screen-reader users hear disjointed numbers (8.8, 92) without the associated semantic labels (Cumulative GPA, Attendance).Cognitive Accessibility: Displaying an unformatted 28-character technical hash string creates cognitive overload and visual confusion for non-technical users.Color Contrast Failures: Subtle gray badges (e.g., #9CA3AF on white cards) fall below WCAG 2.1 AA 4.5:1 contrast requirements, rendering critical status tags illegible in outdoor or high-glare environments.Keyboard Traps & Inoperability: Tabbing through the document focuses on the Add Skill element, but pressing Enter or Space yields no response or focus shift, trapping assistive technology users in dead interaction loops.Responsive Breakpoint AnalysisMobile (375px):The header metadata block wraps unevenly, pushing the university logo off-center.Long text strings (such as the document hash and university affiliation) overflow their container boundaries or wrap awkwardly across three lines.Tap targets for buttons are insufficiently spaced, violating the 48x48px minimum touch standard.Tablet (768px):Spacing collapses between the student biographical summary and the metrics container.Desktop (1440px):Content stretches into a wide container with excessive line lengths, reducing reading comprehension for multi-sentence descriptions.13.
